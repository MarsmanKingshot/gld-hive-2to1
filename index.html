How to read the posters

  • Orientation as in the game once you turn the map so the two traps sit on one line: Bear Trap 2 on the left, Bear Trap 1 on the right, the HQ above the block. Every cell shows the seat code, the X:Y to teleport to, and the march time to its own trap.
  • Ring 1 touches the trap: rally leaders, strongest rally first. Ring 2: the strongest joiners. Rings 3 and 4: everyone else. The middle rows between the hives reach either trap within about 30 s, so they suit the members who voted both.
  • Spare cells are on territory and free; they take newcomers or members who change their mind.
  • Green squares are banners that stand today; a gold square is a banner the design adds.

Keeping the traps or moving them

Around a 3 by 3 trap the first three rings hold at most 60 cities on the 2-tile lattice, wherever the trap stands, so 64 or more seats for Trap 2 always puts some in ring 4 at 25 to 30 s. With the mines permanent, each trap on its current site loses one first-ring cell (742:459 and 746:479): eleven lead seats instead of twelve. A search over every trap pair on today's territory found nothing better than the current sites with two added banners (designs 1 to 3). Letting the banners move as well finds design 4: both traps on mine-free ground, twelve lead seats each, every seat within 28 s, at the price of nineteen new banners, five taken down and everyone teleporting again. The HQ stays in every design.
