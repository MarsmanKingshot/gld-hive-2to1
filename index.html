How to read the posters

  • Orientation as in the game once you turn the map so the two traps sit on one line: Bear Trap 2 on the left, Bear Trap 1 on the right, the HQ above the block. Every cell shows the seat code, the X:Y to teleport to, and the march time to its own trap.
  • Ring 1 touches the trap: rally leaders, strongest rally first. Ring 2: the strongest joiners. Rings 3 and 4: everyone else. The middle rows between the hives reach either trap within about 30 s, so they suit the members who voted both.
  • Spare cells are on territory and free; they take newcomers or members who change their mind.
  • Green squares are banners that stand today; a gold square is a banner the design adds.

Why the traps, the HQ and the banners do not move

Around a 3 by 3 trap the first three rings hold at most 60 cities on the 2-tile lattice, wherever the trap stands. 66 seats for Trap 2 therefore always means six or more in ring 4, at 25 to 30 s. Moving Trap 2 gains nothing, and moving Trap 1 north or south only puts today's banners on top of its inner rings (three to eight ring cells lost). The thirteen banners built for the hive all keep their purpose in every design.

For the officers

  • Design 1 costs nothing and can start today. Its price is the band: eleven Trap 2 members sit in the two middle rows at 25 to 34 s from their trap, six of them over 30 s, in what used to be Trap 1's outer rows.
  • Design 2 needs one banner on free ground beside the lake (Mapper terrain says x 732 to 735 is clear at y 473 to 484; check in the game before building). Only six Trap 2 seats are over 27 s (the farthest is the corner seat 750:485 at 31 s, as today) and Trap 1 keeps its second ring.
  • Design 3 keeps everyone within 31 s with nothing built, but asks 6 of the 23 undecided to take Trap 1: 60 / 40 instead of 67 / 33.
  • In every design the lead seats (ring 1) of both traps are the same twelve cells as on the current poster; only the outer rings change hands. Members already seated near the trap they voted for keep their seat.
  • The nodes under seats, the trap rebuilds and the old banners to take down are as on the officers' page of the current site.
