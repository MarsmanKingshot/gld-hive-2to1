Three things before the move

1. Clear the tiles

Use the Clearance Tool on whatever stands where the traps go: Bear Trap 1 takes x 758 to 760, y 459 to 461; Bear Trap 2 takes x 748 to 750, y 469 to 471. The seats are on free ground.

2. Build the traps

The alliance can have two bear traps, so each old trap comes down before its new one goes up. Do it right after that bear has fired, so no rally is lost. Before you demolish, open the old trap's panel and note its level and whether a rebuilt trap keeps it.

Place the trap so that its bottom-left tile is that coordinate; it is three by three tiles. The poster below shows the traps with the same numbers.

3. Check the seating

88 members have a seat: everyone who voted, the strongest nearest their trap, ring 1 being the rally leads. The 10 members without a seat did not vote; they pick one of the 13 free seats on the seat page, or one of the 33 free cells further out. Check the workbook: swap names where you know better, write the name of anyone who picked a free seat, and send it back; the pages redraw from it.

After the move

Once both traps stand, everyone teleports to their seat, ring 1 first. When nobody lives in the old block any more, these nine banners have no job left and can come down to free banner slots: 737:483, 738:463, 738:476, 739:456, 741:470, 743:487, 744:480, 746:451, 746:458. The other pale red banners on the banner map, the old cluster further out, can follow when slots are needed. The purple banners are the corridors out of the hive and stay.
